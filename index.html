Data & transformations

Source: World Bank World Development Indicators, via light-and-salt/World-Bank-Data-by-Indicators. scripts/prepare_data.py builds data/wb_clean.csv:

  • Joins GDP per capita (constant 2010 US$), CO₂ per capita and total, population, life expectancy, energy use, renewable share and urban share by country and year.
  • Keeps only countries; regional and income aggregates (“World”, “High income”, …) are dropped.
  • Life expectancy is the average of the female and male series (no total series in the source).
  • CO₂ data ends in 2018, so charts stop there.
